## 9. Rolling origin and limitations of the evaluation

Baselines need no fitting, so they are evaluated on *every* 30-day window of the last 30% of each project (val + test): a true out-of-time evaluation with about twice the FirstFail runs of the test split alone.

| Subset | Best simple baseline over all windows | Macro APFDc | Runs |
|---|---|---|---|
| All | ChangedOrNewFirst | 0.859 | 18149 |
| FirstFail-asof | QTF | 0.835 | 737 |
| FirstFail-old | QTF | 0.814 | 479 |
| New/Edited | ChangedOrNewFirst | 0.986 | 1990 |

The best baseline per subset is the same as on the test split, so the bars the models are measured against do not depend on the choice of origin. **Models were evaluated at a single origin** (the test split): refitting the pooled model for every 30-day window of every project did not fit the compute budget. This is a stated limitation.
